PDF to Word

Extract the text of a PDF into an editable Word (.docx) document. Free, no sign-up, no uploads.

    How it works

    This tool reads the text of your PDF page by page and puts it into a Word document so you can edit it. Complex layouts, tables and images are not reproduced exactly — the goal is quickly editable text.

    Scanned (image-only) PDFs have no selectable text and can't be converted.

    🔒 Your files are never uploaded to a server — everything happens in your browser, on your own device. That makes it fast and private.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the layout preserved?

    Text and page order are kept; fonts, tables and images are not reproduced.

    Are my files safe?

    Yes. Files are never uploaded — processing happens entirely on your device.

Final Grade Calculator

Find out what score you need on your final exam to reach your target grade, or calculate your overall grade.

How it works

Your overall grade is current grade × its weight + final score × final weight. To find the score you need: needed = (target − current × w) / (1 − w). With a current grade of 82% worth 75% of the course and a target of 85%, you need 94% on the final.

If your course has a minimum final exam requirement, enter it as well. If you already know your final score, enter it to see your overall grade.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the grade I need on my final?

Needed = (target − current × current weight) / final weight.

What if I need more than 100%?

Then the target isn't reachable with the final alone — ask about extra credit or a lower target.

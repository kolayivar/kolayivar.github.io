VAT Calculator

Add VAT to a net price or remove VAT from a gross price instantly. Works with any VAT or sales tax rate. Free online VAT calculator.

Enter your country's VAT / sales tax rate, e.g. 20 for the UK.

How it works

Value Added Tax (VAT) is a consumption tax added to the price of goods and services. Gross price = Net price × (1 + VAT rate / 100). For example, adding 20% VAT to a net price of 1,000 gives 200 in VAT and a gross price of 1,200.

To remove VAT from a gross price, divide by (1 + rate/100): 1,200 / 1.20 = 1,000. A common mistake is simply subtracting 20% from the gross price, which gives the wrong answer. This calculator also works for US sales tax and GST — just enter your local rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add VAT to a price?

Multiply the net price by 1 + rate/100. With 20% VAT: 500 × 1.20 = 600.

How do I remove VAT from a price?

Divide the gross price by 1 + rate/100. With 20% VAT: 600 / 1.20 = 500.

Does this work for sales tax and GST?

Yes. The math is the same — enter your sales tax or GST rate in the rate field.

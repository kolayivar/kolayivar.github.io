Ideal Weight Calculator

Find your ideal body weight for your height using four classic formulas plus the healthy BMI range.

How it works

There is no single correct ideal-weight formula. This tool shows four classic clinical formulas (Devine, Robinson, Miller, Hamwi) together with the WHO healthy BMI range (18.5 – 24.9).

The formulas use only height and sex and ignore muscle mass, frame size and age. A healthy range is more meaningful than one number.

For information only — talk to a doctor about your health.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which formula is most accurate?

None is definitive; the healthy BMI range is the most reliable guide.

Unit Converter

Convert between meters, feet, inches, miles, kilograms, pounds, Celsius, Fahrenheit, acres, liters, gallons and more.

How it works

Convert length (meters, kilometers, centimeters, miles, yards, feet, inches), weight (kilograms, grams, tonnes, pounds, ounces), temperature (°C, °F, K), area (m², hectares, acres, ft²), volume (liters, mL, m³, US gallons, fl oz, cups) and speed (km/h, m/s, mph, knots).

Common conversions: 1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 foot = 30.48 cm, 1 mile = 1.609 km, 1 lb = 0.4536 kg, °F = °C × 9/5 + 32.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32. For example, 25 °C = 77 °F.

How many centimeters are in an inch?

Exactly 2.54 cm.

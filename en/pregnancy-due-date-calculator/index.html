Pregnancy Due Date Calculator

Enter the first day of your last period to estimate your due date, how many weeks pregnant you are and your trimester.

How it works

Due dates are usually estimated with Naegele's rule: add 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP). Pregnancy weeks are counted from the LMP, so conception typically happens around week 2.

Only a small share of babies arrive on their exact due date — birth between weeks 38 and 42 is considered normal. An ultrasound can give a more precise estimate.

For information only. Always follow your doctor's or midwife's guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are pregnancy weeks counted?

From the first day of your last period, not from conception.

How accurate is the due date?

It's an estimate. Most babies are born within two weeks before or after it.

Age Calculator

Find your exact age in years, months and days, total days lived, the weekday you were born and days until your next birthday.

How it works

Your exact age is the difference between your date of birth and a target date, counted in whole years, then remaining months and days. Leap years and different month lengths are handled automatically.

Change the target date to find out how old you'll be on a future date — useful for retirement, school enrollment or eligibility checks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate my age on a future date?

Yes — just change the 'Age at date' field.

How are leap years handled?

Automatically. The calculator uses real calendar dates, including February 29.

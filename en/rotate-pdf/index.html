Rotate PDF

Rotate upside-down or sideways PDF pages by 90°, 180° or 270°. All pages or only the ones you choose.

    How it works

    Scanned pages often come out sideways or upside down. Pick an angle; leave the pages field empty to rotate all pages, or enter something like 2, 4-6.

    🔒 Your files are never uploaded to a server — everything happens in your browser, on your own device. That makes it fast and private.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does rotating reduce quality?

    No — pages aren't re-rendered, only their orientation changes.

    Are my files safe?

    Yes. Files are never uploaded — processing happens entirely on your device.

Merge PDF

Combine multiple PDF files into one, in any order. Free, unlimited and private — files never leave your browser.

    How it works

    Select or drag in the PDF files you want to combine, reorder them with the arrows and click Merge PDFs. Pages are copied as-is, with no quality loss.

    Perfect for applications, invoices, assignments and scanned documents.

    🔒 Your files are never uploaded to a server — everything happens in your browser, on your own device. That makes it fast and private.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How many PDFs can I merge?

    There's no fixed limit — only your device's memory.

    Are my files safe?

    Yes. Files are never uploaded — processing happens entirely on your device.

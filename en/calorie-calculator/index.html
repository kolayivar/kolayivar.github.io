Calorie Calculator

Estimate your basal metabolic rate (BMR) and daily calorie needs (TDEE) from age, height, weight and activity level.

How it works

Basal metabolic rate (BMR) is the energy your body uses at rest. This calculator uses the widely used Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) is BMR multiplied by an activity factor.

Results are estimates; muscle mass, hormones and health conditions affect your real needs.

For information only. Consult a dietitian or doctor for a nutrition plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR is energy used at rest; TDEE adds your daily activity.

Which formula is used?

The Mifflin-St Jeor equation.

Percentage Calculator

Calculate percentages instantly: X% of Y, what percent X is of Y, percentage increase and decrease. Free online percentage calculator.

How it works

A percentage expresses a part of a whole as a fraction of 100. To find X% of a number, multiply the number by X and divide by 100: 20% of 250 = 250 × 20 / 100 = 50. To find what percent one number is of another, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100.

Percentage change is calculated as (New − Old) / Old × 100. If a price rises from $200 to $250, that is a 25% increase. These formulas are used for discounts, raises, grades, interest, taxes and statistics.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 80 is 80 × 15 / 100 = 12.

How is percentage increase calculated?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100.

How do I add a percentage to a number?

Multiply the number by (1 + rate/100). Adding 18% to 500 gives 500 × 1.18 = 590.

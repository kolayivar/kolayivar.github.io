Case Converter

Convert text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case instantly. Copy the result with one click.

How it works

Accidentally typed with Caps Lock on? Need a headline in Title Case? Paste your text, pick a style and copy the result with one click.

Sentence case capitalizes the first letter of each sentence; Title Case capitalizes the first letter of every word. Everything runs in your browser, so your text stays private.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text stored anywhere?

No — conversion happens locally in your browser.

What is Sentence case?

Only the first letter of each sentence is capitalized; everything else is lowercase.

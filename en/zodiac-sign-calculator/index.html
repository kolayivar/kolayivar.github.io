Zodiac Sign Calculator

Enter your date of birth to find your sun sign, its element and date range instantly.

How it works

Your sun sign is set by the constellation the Sun was in on your birthday: Aries (Mar 21 – Apr 19), Taurus (Apr 20 – May 20), Gemini (May 21 – Jun 20), Cancer (Jun 21 – Jul 22), Leo (Jul 23 – Aug 22), Virgo (Aug 23 – Sep 22), Libra (Sep 23 – Oct 22), Scorpio (Oct 23 – Nov 21), Sagittarius (Nov 22 – Dec 21), Capricorn (Dec 22 – Jan 19), Aquarius (Jan 20 – Feb 18), Pisces (Feb 19 – Mar 20).

If you were born on a cusp day, your sign can vary by year and birth time. Rising signs require your exact birth time and place.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my zodiac sign determined?

By which date range your birthday falls into.

Does it calculate rising signs?

No — a rising sign needs your exact birth time and location.

Dimensional Weight Calculator

Calculate the dimensional (volumetric) weight of a parcel and the chargeable weight for shipping.

How it works

Dimensional (volumetric) weight lets couriers charge for bulky but light parcels: L × W × H (cm) / divisor. Most international couriers use a divisor of 5000; some use 4000 or 6000.

You're billed on the greater of the dimensional weight and the actual weight — the chargeable weight.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is dimensional weight calculated?

Multiply length × width × height in cm and divide by the courier's divisor (often 5000).

Am I charged by actual or dimensional weight?

By whichever is greater.

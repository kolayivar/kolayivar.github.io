Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Data you enter

All calculations run in your browser; the data you enter is never sent to our servers. We do not collect, store or share the information you type into our calculators.

Cookies and advertising

Google Analytics loads only if you allow analytics in the privacy choices panel. Choose “Necessary only” to reject it; you can change your choice with the “Cookie settings” button at the bottom of the page. AdSense advertising is currently disabled on this site and no advertising cookies are loaded. Before ads are enabled, the site must receive AdSense approval, use a Google-certified consent management platform, and meet the commercial licensing terms for every data source it uses.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Learn more about how Google uses data.

Analytics

Google Analytics measures page and session activity only if you allow analytics. It is not loaded when you reject analytics. Text, calculator values, files, game scores, plan goals and search terms are not sent to analytics. Usage events contain only limited information such as feature type and a general result count. You can withdraw consent at any time using the “Cookie settings” button at the bottom of the page. You can also use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Weather

For weather searches, the city name you enter and the selected city coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo geocoding and forecast services. Browser location permission is not used. The free Open-Meteo API is limited to non-commercial use; advertising-supported sites count as commercial use, so advertising remains disabled until the required commercial licence is in place. See the Open-Meteo terms.

Recently used tools

The “Pick up where you left off” list on the home page stores only the page links and titles of tools you opened, in your browser on your device. It does not store calculator values, files or text entered into forms. You can clear this list with the “Clear history” button or by clearing browser data.

Third-party hosted code

The PDF merge, split and rotate tools load the required PDF-lib JavaScript library from the jsDelivr CDN. The CDN may receive technical connection information such as an IP address, browser type and requested file. PDF document contents are processed in your browser and are not sent to jsDelivr to load the library. See jsDelivr privacy information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Data you enter

All calculations run in your browser; the data you enter is never sent to our servers. We do not collect, store or share the information you type into our calculators.

Cookies and advertising

Optional analytics and advertising technologies load only if you allow them in the privacy choices panel. Choose “Necessary only” to reject them; you can change your choice with the “Cookie settings” button at the bottom of the page. If advertising is enabled, third-party services such as Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies. Before Google ads are shown in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, consent must be collected through a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP); AdSense is not loaded while that platform is unavailable.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Learn more about how Google uses data.

Analytics

Google Analytics measures page and session activity only if you allow analytics. It is not loaded when you reject analytics. Text, calculator values, files, game scores, plan goals and search terms are not sent to analytics. Usage events contain only limited information such as feature type and a general result count. You can withdraw consent at any time using the “Cookie settings” button at the bottom of the page. You can also use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Weather

For weather searches, the city name you enter and the selected city coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo geocoding and forecast services. Browser location permission is not used. The free Open-Meteo API is limited to non-commercial use; advertising-supported sites count as commercial use, so a commercial licence is required before enabling ads on the weather feature. See the Open-Meteo terms.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page.

Strong Password Generator

Generate strong, random, secure passwords with custom length, letters, numbers and symbols. Created locally on your device.

How it works

A strong password is long, random and unique for every account. Aim for at least 12–16 characters mixing letters, numbers and symbols. This tool uses your browser's cryptographically secure random generator (crypto.getRandomValues) — passwords are never sent anywhere.

Look-alike characters (0/O, 1/l/I) are excluded to avoid confusion. Store passwords in a password manager and turn on two-factor authentication.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these passwords safe?

Yes. They're generated on your device with a cryptographically secure random generator and never transmitted.

How long should a password be?

At least 12 characters; 16 or more is better.

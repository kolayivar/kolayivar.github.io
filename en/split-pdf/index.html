Split PDF

Extract selected pages from a PDF or split every page into its own file. Free and private.

    How it works

    Type a range like 1-3, 5, 8- to keep pages 1–3, page 5 and page 8 to the end in a new PDF. Choose "Every page as a separate PDF" to download all pages as individual files in a ZIP.

    🔒 Your files are never uploaded to a server — everything happens in your browser, on your own device. That makes it fast and private.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I write a page range?

    Separate with commas: 2-4, 7, 10-. A trailing dash means 'to the end'.

    Are my files safe?

    Yes. Files are never uploaded — processing happens entirely on your device.

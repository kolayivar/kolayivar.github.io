Pay Raise Calculator

Calculate your new salary after a percentage raise, or find the raise percentage from your old and new pay.

How it works

New salary = current salary × (1 + raise / 100). To find the raise percentage: (new − old) / old × 100.

Add the inflation rate to see your real raise: (1 + raise) / (1 + inflation) − 1. A negative real raise means your purchasing power fell even though your pay went up.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a real raise?

Your raise adjusted for inflation — how much your purchasing power actually changed.

How do I calculate a raise percentage?

(New salary − old salary) / old salary × 100.

Word to PDF

Save your Word (.docx) document as a PDF in seconds. Headings, bold/italic text and tables are preserved.

    How it works

    Choose your Word file and a preview is prepared. Then click Save as PDF and pick "Save as PDF" as the destination. Headings, paragraphs, bold/italic/underline, lists and tables are kept.

    Save old .doc files as .docx first. Images are not included in this version.

    🔒 Your files are never uploaded to a server — everything happens in your browser, on your own device. That makes it fast and private.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does it work with .doc files?

    Only .docx. Use 'Save As → .docx' in Word first.

    Are my files safe?

    Yes. Files are never uploaded — processing happens entirely on your device.

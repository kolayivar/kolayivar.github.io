Fuel Cost Calculator

Estimate fuel used and trip cost from distance, consumption (L/100 km) and fuel price, split per person.

How it works

Trip cost = distance × consumption / 100 × price per liter. A 450 km trip in a car using 6.5 L/100 km burns 29.25 liters.

Real consumption varies with speed, load, A/C, traffic and terrain. Check your trip computer for your car's actual average.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my car's L/100 km?

Fill the tank, reset the trip meter, then divide liters used at the next fill-up by km driven and multiply by 100.

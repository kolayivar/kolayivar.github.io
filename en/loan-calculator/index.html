Calculate your monthly loan payment, total repayment and total interest for personal, auto or mortgage loans. Free loan calculator.

Enter the annual percentage rate (APR).

How it works

Most loans use an amortizing payment schedule with equal monthly payments: Payment = Principal × r / (1 − (1 + r)−n), where r is the monthly interest rate (APR / 12) and n is the number of months. Early payments are mostly interest; later payments mostly reduce the principal.

A longer term lowers your monthly payment but increases the total interest you pay. Use this calculator to compare terms and rates before you borrow. Actual payments may also include fees, insurance or taxes depending on your lender.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a monthly loan payment calculated?

With the amortization formula using the principal, monthly rate (APR/12) and number of months. This calculator does it for you.

Is a longer loan term better?

It lowers the monthly payment but increases total interest. The shortest term you can afford is usually the cheapest.

Why is my lender's payment different?

Lenders may add fees, insurance or taxes, or use a slightly different rate convention. Check your official repayment schedule.

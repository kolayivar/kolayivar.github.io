Discount Calculator

Find the sale price and how much you save. Supports stacked discounts (e.g. 30% off plus an extra 10%).

How it works

The sale price is Price × (1 − discount / 100). A $150 item at 30% off costs $105, saving you $45.

Stacked discounts don't add up: 30% off followed by an extra 10% off is a 37% total discount, not 40%. Use the extra discount field to see the real final price.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 30% + 10% off the same as 40% off?

No — it's 37% off, because the second discount applies to the already reduced price.

How do I find the discount percentage?

(Original − sale price) / original × 100. Our percentage calculator can also do this.
